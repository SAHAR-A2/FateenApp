## 3. Certification summary

- **Repository:** 68 tables (23 lookup, 8 canonical, 8 translation,
  8 relationship, 11 history, 6 audit, 4 search), 195 FKs (all RESTRICT),
  206 indexes, 64 triggers (53 `set_updated_at` + 11 `prevent_history_mutation`),
  2 functions, 10 enums, 3 extensions, 31 migrations.
- **Graphs:** dependency graph acyclic; every FK target exists; every FK column
  indexed; no orphans; no circular dependencies; no duplicates; no CASCADE; no
  `is_active`; single lifecycle vocabulary.
- **Conventions:** naming/typing/identifier/constraint conventions all audited
  PASS; ADRs 001–008 complied; deviations are the three documented ones
  (BIGINT identity, immutable-history no `updated_at`/`deleted_at`,
  derived read models without governance columns).
- **Documentation:** entity inventory (68 tables), dependency/migration graph,
  conventions, ADRs, mission reports 03/04/05/07/08/09, open-questions log —
  synchronized and verified by the sync check.
